How a rating
is built.

Understand what a number includes before using it to make a scouting decision.

Compare like with like

Stats are adjusted for champion and matchup, then compared within the player's league.

Account for the league

League strength places performance on a shared scale. A strong local number is not automatically a strong cross-league rating.

Show uncertainty

Tiers and ranges show where the data can and cannot distinguish players. Missing evidence stays missing.

The private application contains Method and evidence, benchmark definitions and limitations. Retrospective simulations are not a live track record. LPL is not rated. Solo queue and draft tools are not part of the current scouting product.

For players and teams

What is this for?

A shortlist aid for professional scouting. Combine it with VOD review, tryouts, team needs and your own judgement.

What does it leave out?

It does not establish contract availability, communication, personality, team fit or whether a player should be signed. A rating is an opinion derived from public match statistics.

Can a player flag an error?

Yes. Write to can@flexpick.gg with the player and the detail that needs checking.
