Website Privacy Notice

With this privacy notice we inform you about how Flexpick processes your personal data and about your rights under data protection law. This notice applies to professional and semi-professional League of Legends players whose data Flexpick uses for its ratings, to customers and people who request access to Flexpick, and to visitors of our website.

Responsible entity / data controller

Flexpick is a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak), registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 42205684 and located at Van Walbeeckstraat 48H, 1058CT, Amsterdam, the Netherlands (“Flexpick”, “we”, “us”). Flexpick is responsible for the collection and processing of your personal data (“data controller”).

Flexpick provides ratings, rankings and scouting reports on League of Legends players to professional teams and player agencies (“customers”). Customers access full player profiles through a private, login-only area of our website.

Sources of personal data and relevant data

Players. Flexpick mainly processes personal data that it has obtained from publicly accessible sources. These are public esports match statistics databases and websites, community-run esports wikis, public websites that list which ranked accounts belong to professional players, League of Legends game data services, and your own public profiles, such as your social media bio. In addition, Flexpick processes data received from third parties, such as teams and player agents, and data you provide to us yourself, for example when you contact us. On request, Flexpick will inform you of the specific sources used for your personal data. Data that teams make available to Flexpick from GRID, the official esports data platform, is not a source for Flexpick’s own ratings (see section “GRID data processed on behalf of teams”).

Customers. Flexpick processes personal data that it receives from you or from your organisation when you request access, use our services or contact us.

Website visitors. Flexpick processes technical data that is collected automatically when you visit our website.

To provide our services and comply with applicable laws, we may process the following categories of personal data:

  • Player identification data (such as in-game name, real name where publicly known, nationality, country of residence, age, year or date of birth);
  • Competitive career data (such as team, league, role, teams and leagues played in, contract or free-agent status where publicly known);
  • Match performance data (such as statistics from official competitive matches);
  • Ranked game data (such as ranked accounts publicly linked to professional players, rank and statistics from ranked games);
  • Rating and assessment data (such as ratings, rankings, role and playstyle labels, scouting notes and comparisons with similar players);
  • Public profile data (such as links to public social media profiles);
  • Customer contact and account data (such as name, e-mail address, organisation, role and licence);
  • Authentication and access data (such as login records and requests for one-time login codes);
  • Contract and billing data (such as licence details, invoices and payment status);
  • Communication data (such as e-mails, feedback and requests);
  • Website technical data (such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, time of visit and strictly necessary cookies).

Flexpick does not process special categories of personal data, such as health data. Flexpick does not use GRID data or private practice data of teams (scrims) in its ratings.

Purposes and legal bases for data processing

Flexpick processes your personal data for the following purposes and bases its processing on the following legal grounds in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”).

Based on performance of contractual obligations

  • Performance of pre-contractual measures, such as handling requests for access, demos and trials;
  • Provision of our services to customers, including access to the private area of our website, player profiles, lists, shortlists and scouting reports;
  • Administration of licences and user accounts of customers;
  • Communication with customers about our services.

Where your personal data is processed for the performance of contractual obligations and you do not provide such data, Flexpick may not be able to enter into or perform the contract or give you access to our services.

Based on legitimate interests of Flexpick

  • Calculation of ratings and rankings of players based on their competitive and ranked performance;
  • Making player profiles, lists and reports available to customers for scouting and recruitment;
  • Publication of limited public content about the competitive scene on our website and social media;
  • Checking and improving the accuracy of our ratings, including the use of feedback from customers;
  • Ensuring the security of our website and private area and preventing unauthorised access or sharing of our content;
  • Establishment, exercise and defence of legal claims.

Our legitimate interests are running a scouting service for the esports sector, the interest of teams and player agencies in finding and assessing players, and the interest of players in being discovered by teams. Flexpick has balanced these interests against the interests and rights of players. The data relates to the public competitive activity of players and is largely publicly available. Flexpick limits the data to what is needed for its ratings and offers an easy way to object. A summary of this assessment is available on request.

If Flexpick processes your data according to its legitimate interests, you have the right to object. For more details, see below section “Your rights”.

Based on consent

For certain purposes, of which you will be informed accordingly, Flexpick may process your personal data based on your consent, for example a newsletter subscription. You can withdraw your consent at any time for the future, without giving reasons, by using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us via e-mail at can@flexpick.gg.

Based on legal requirements

  • Issuing invoices and keeping business records in accordance with Dutch tax law;
  • Responding to lawful requests from public authorities;
  • Handling data protection requests and personal data breaches.

Ratings, profiling and automated processing

Flexpick calculates player ratings automatically on the basis of match statistics. This is a form of profiling. The model compares the statistics of a player with those of other players in the same role, takes into account the strength of the league and of opponents, and considers results in direct match-ups.

Ratings are estimates of in-game performance. They are not statements of fact and not a judgement about a player as a person, and they may be inaccurate. Flexpick does not take decisions about players. Customers make their own decisions, and our customer terms require them not to rely on our ratings alone. Flexpick does not use your personal data for decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you.

If you believe that your data or your rating is based on incorrect information, please contact us. Flexpick will review it and correct factual errors.

GRID data processed on behalf of teams

Professional teams receive access to official match data, practice data (scrims) and related player data through GRID, the official esports data platform. A team can instruct Flexpick to analyse the data from its own GRID account. In that case the team is the data controller and Flexpick acts as its data processor. This means that Flexpick processes the data only on behalf of the team, only on its documented instructions and on the basis of a data processing agreement with the team.

Flexpick keeps the GRID data of each team separate. Flexpick uses it only to provide analyses to that team. Flexpick does not use GRID data for its own ratings, for other customers or for public content, and does not combine it with the data of other teams. At the end of the service, Flexpick deletes the GRID data or returns it to the team, as the team instructs.

For this processing, the privacy notice of the team applies. If you wish to exercise your rights regarding GRID data, please contact the team concerned. If you contact Flexpick, we will forward your request to the team and support it in responding.

Website and cookies

Our website is hosted by an external service provider. When you visit it, technical data such as your IP address is processed to display the website and to protect it against misuse. Flexpick does not use analytics, advertising or session-recording tools. Flexpick only uses cookies that are strictly necessary, for example to log customers into the private area and to protect the website against automated attacks. Should Flexpick introduce analytics in the future, these will only run after you have given your consent.

Recipients of data

Within Flexpick, only the owner has access to your personal data.

Customers, being professional teams and player agencies with a Flexpick licence, receive player data through the private area of our website and in reports. Our customer terms oblige them to keep this data confidential and not to share it further.

Furthermore, trusted third-party service providers contracted by Flexpick may receive data for the aforementioned purposes. In particular, these are providers of website hosting and security, login and access control, storage of code and project files, domain and e-mail services, payment services and software tools. These providers process data on our behalf on the basis of a data processing agreement.

Where required by law or upon a lawful request, we may disclose data to public bodies such as tax authorities, courts and law enforcement agencies. In the event of a sale or transfer of the business, personal data may be disclosed to potential or actual purchasers and their advisors, subject to confidentiality and data protection safeguards.

Data retention

Flexpick processes and retains your personal data as long as this is necessary for the purposes described in this notice and for the fulfilment of its contractual and legal obligations.

Data transfers to third countries

Flexpick may transfer personal data to service providers and customers outside the European Economic Area (EEA), in particular in the United States. Flexpick transfers personal data outside the EEA only insofar as this is necessary for the purposes described in this notice, there is a legal basis for the data transfer and, if no adequacy decision of the European Commission exists, appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses are implemented. For service providers in the United States that are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Flexpick relies on the corresponding adequacy decision.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data as well as receive a copy of your data;
  • rectification and erasure;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability, where the processing is based on a contract or on consent;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where the processing is based on consent;
  • object, especially if the data is processed pursuant to the legitimate interests of Flexpick;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes applicable data protection laws. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). You may also lodge a complaint in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement.

Right to object

Where Flexpick processes your data on the basis of its legitimate interests, you have the right to object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. This includes the profiling of players for our ratings. If you object, Flexpick will no longer process your data unless it can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds. In practice, Flexpick will remove you from its ratings. To object, send an e-mail to can@flexpick.gg stating your in-game name.

Data protection requests

Should you have any questions related to data protection or wish to exercise your rights, please send an e-mail to: can@flexpick.gg.

Changes to this privacy notice

Flexpick may update this privacy notice from time to time to reflect changes in its services or in applicable law. The version date at the top of this notice shows the latest version.
