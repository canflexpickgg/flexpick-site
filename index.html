No number without its sample size.

Every list shows the games it covers and the patch range. Ranks come with tiers and ranges, because neighbours on a list are rarely a real difference. When the data is missing, we say so.

How a rating is built

  1. Champion and matchupEvery stat is compared with what the same champion normally produces that season, lane leads with the exact matchup.
  2. The player's ownStats are weighed by whether they follow a player to a new team and go with later results, not by how they look on a scoreboard.
  3. League strengthEach league gets a level from how players' numbers change when they move between leagues and from head-to-head results across leagues. Every level carries an error range.
  4. Tiers and rangesThe rating becomes a tier and a range of ranks, taken from how far ratings really move from one season to the next.

What it is for

  • Deciding who to look at. A shortlist to take into video review, interviews and tryouts.
  • Comparing across leagues. A regional-league player and a top-league player on one scale.
  • Asking better questions. Where a player is strong against his own league, and where he is not.

What it does not do

  • No betting or gambling use.
  • No raw match data. Flexpick shows derived ratings only.
  • No verdict on a person. It knows nothing about communication, attitude, contracts or how someone plays in scrims.
  • No LPL and no solo queue ratings today.

For players

Flexpick uses statistics from public professional matches, together with a player's in-game name, team, league, role, nationality and age as published. It does not collect contact details, private accounts or anything from outside professional play. Ratings are produced by a statistical model and are one input among many for the teams that read them; Flexpick makes no decisions about anyone.

If you are a player and want to see, correct or remove your data, or object to its use, write to can@flexpick.gg. Requests are answered within one month and are free. Requests about players under 18 are honoured without asking for reasons.
