Before you start

What can I see in the preview?

Real public lists with most identities hidden. Private ratings, full player profiles and team history require access.

Do I need a password?

No. Private access uses the existing Cloudflare email-code login. Your email must already be authorised.

Does a higher rating guarantee a better signing?

No. Use the tier, range, sample and profile together, then combine the evidence with your own scouting.
